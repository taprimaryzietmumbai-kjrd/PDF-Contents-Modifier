How it works

  1. Open your PDF.
  2. Click a text item to edit it.
  3. Use Cover / Erase for old text or images that need replacing.
  4. Add replacement text/image.
  5. Export the modified PDF.

Everything runs locally in your browser. The PDF is not uploaded to a server.
